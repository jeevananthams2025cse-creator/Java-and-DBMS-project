Donor Eligibility Policy

Under clinical safety standards, blood donors must observe a minimum rest period of 90 days between whole blood donation events.

• The server automatically calculates days since last donation.
• Ineligible attempts will be safely blocked.

Shelf Life Rules

Whole blood units remain biologically viable for 31 days from collection. Expiry dates are automatically tagged and tracked in inventory.
